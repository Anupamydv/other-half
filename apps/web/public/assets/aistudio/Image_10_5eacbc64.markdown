## Brand & Style

This design system blends the hospitality-forward warmth of Airbnb with the kinetic, empowering social discovery mechanics of Bumble BFF. It serves young professionals, digital nomads, and urban relocators navigating shared living. The emotional tone must communicate psychological safety, genuine compatibility, and vibrant communal energy. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Softness:
- **Tone:** Welcoming, trustworthy, vibrant, and clear.
- **Visual Stance:** Generous negative space, gentle organic roundness, warm canvas surfaces (avoiding stark blue-tinted tech whites), and structured visual cards that make personality compatibility, personal habits, and home aesthetics instantly readable.
- **Philosophy:** Human connection over cold transactional real estate. Trust signals (identity verification, background checks, mutual friend connections) sit proudly beside personality badges (early riser, pet lover, social battery index).

## Colors

The palette avoids clinical grays and chilly tech blues in favor of sunlit, domestic hues that immediately reduce the stress of apartment hunting and roommate matchmaking:

- **Primary (`#F05A4A` - Ember Coral):** Used for primary conversion points, discovery action buttons, active matching indicators, and celebratory highlights.
- **Secondary (`#E08738` - Sunbaked Amber):** Complements the primary in gradient overlays, lifestyle tag highlights, and mutual interest signals.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Trust):** Dedicated exclusively to high-confidence signals: identity verification checkmarks, verified lease credentials, background screening shields, and high compatibility percentages (e.g., "96% Compatible").
- **Neutral (`#76726B` - Warm Sandstone Neutral):** Forms the backbone of canvas surfaces, borders, and warm muted states.

### Surface System
- **Base Background:** `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Linen) ensures long browsing sessions remain gentle on the eyes.
- **Card / Surface Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White) layered atop the linen background for crisp content definition.
- **Secondary Surface:** `#F4F2EE` (Warm Oat) for input fields, inactive chips, and nested lifestyle modules.

### Typography Colors
- **Text Headings / Deep Slate:** `#1E293B` provides high-contrast, accessible legibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Text Muted / Neutral Slate:** `#64748B` for secondary specs, roommate bio excerpts, and timestamps.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the single typographic voice across all display, body, and UI label tiers. Its geometric skeleton paired with subtle warm apertures reflects human sociability and technological clarity simultaneously.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for property hero discovery banners, match celebrations, and empty states. Tight negative letter-spacing gives an editorial, grounded feel.
- **Body Styles:** Body copy balances generous line heights (`26px` on `16px` base) to ensure long roommate self-descriptions, lifestyle preferences, and house rules remain effortlessly readable.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Habit tags, compatibility metrics, and bottom bar titles utilize `label-md` and `label-sm` with slightly boosted character spacing for crisp legibility even on compact viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid prioritizes single-handed thumb reach and swipe-based discovery interfaces while expanding fluidly into multi-column split views on larger viewports.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Strategy
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom navigation is pinned; discover cards utilize full-width minus margins with edge-peek horizontal scroll for supplementary metadata.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Transitions swipe cards into dual-column feeds (e.g., Room on left, Roommate profile on right).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout max-capped at `1240px` with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Nav switches to an elevated, floating header; viewports render interactive map-and-profile split view panes.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component inner padding utilizes `space-md` for standard cards and `space-sm` for compact habit chips.
- Grouping related roommate signals (e.g., clean habit badges) follows strict 8px (`space-sm`) gaps to reinforce visual chunking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages sun-drenched, diffuse ambient drop shadows mixed with subtle translucent boundary rims, creating an inviting, tactile layer model:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base surface (`#FAF9F6`).
- **Level 1 (Feed & Content Cards):** 
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`
  - Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Filter Pills):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Shadow: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0px 12px 28px -6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Match Drawers & Bottom Sheets):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Shadow: `0px 10px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12), 0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.10)`
- **Level 4 (Floating Action Discovery & Navigation Dock):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 36px -4px rgba(240, 90, 74, 0.12), 0px 8px 24px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The roundedness language uses Level 3 (Pill-shaped & Highly Curvilinear). Organic, smooth edges create emotional warmth and approachability, breaking free from traditional enterprise real estate grids.

### Corner Radii Tokens
- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded`):** `1rem` (16px) applied to inner detail panes, media carousel viewports, and listing thumbnails.
- **Cards & Profile Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `2rem` (32px) applied to discovery cards, modal sheets, and coliving house overview containers.
- **Pills & Interactive Action Controls (`rounded-xl`):** `9999px` (Fully rounded pills) applied to search bars, buttons, compatibility tags, lifestyle chips, and bottom navigation pill containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Fully rounded pill (`rounded-xl`), Ember Coral (`#F05A4A`) fill, crisp `#FFFFFF` text in `label-lg`. Pressed states transition with a micro-scale down to `0.98` and a subtle glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(240, 90, 74, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Social/Match Action:** Pill button with Oat background (`#F4F2EE`), Deep Slate text (`#1E293B`).
- **Discovery Swipe Triggers:** Circular floating buttons (Pass: `#FFFFFF` with slate icon; Match/Connect: `#F05A4A` with white heart; Super-Connect: `#E08738` with star icon).

### Chips & Lifestyle Badges
- **Habit Chips (e.g., "Early Bird", "Vegetarian", "WFH"):** Compact pills (`px-3 py-1.5`), `#F4F2EE` base, `#1E293B` text in `label-md`. When selected or shared in common with the browsing user, background shifts to a soft Coral tint (`rgba(240, 90, 74, 0.12)`) with `#F05A4A` typography and an icon prefix.
- **Trust Badges:** Pill-shaped badge with light emerald surface (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid `#10B981` text and verified check icon.
- **Compatibility Score Meter:** Pill displaying `94% Match` combining a subtle gradient border from `#F05A4A` to `#E08738` on a pure white backdrop.

### Cards
- **Roommate Profile Card:** Outer container has `rounded-lg` (32px), pure white fill, and Level 1 elevation. Hero visual with 4:5 aspect ratio and rounded interior corners (`rounded`), overlaid at the bottom with profile name, age, profession, and floating Emerald verification pill.
- **Coliving Room Listing Card:** High-resolution photography carousel with pill-shaped pagination dots, followed by headline price, split utilities tag, and mutual roommate micro-avatars stacked with `+3 matches live here`.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed pill-shaped input fields (`height: 52px`, `rounded-xl`) with background `#F4F2EE`, zero heavy borders, and placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focus state transitions background to `#FFFFFF` with a `2px` focus ring in `rgba(240, 90, 74, 0.4)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded-rectangles (`8px` radius), `#F4F2EE` border/fill when unchecked, animating into `#10B981` solid with a white checkmark when verified or selected.
- **Radio Toggles:** Circular with concentric ring scaling in `#F05A4A`.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Message threads and roommate match queues use separated card rows instead of hairline divider lines. Each item floats on a `#FFFFFF` surface with Level 1 elevation, featuring unread pulse dots in `#F05A4A`.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Pinned floating dock elevated above canvas with `Level 4` shadow. Contains four primary icons (Explore, Matches, Messages, Profile) rendered in `#64748B`, transitioning into active filled icons encased in a subtle coral-tinted pill indicator with `#F05A4A` fill.
